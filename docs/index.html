How far can a noisy series honestly be projected?

90 days of real daily air-quality values, cleaned, smoothed two ways, and projected 7 days with a straight line — shown with the uncertainty band the line usually hides.

Dots = daily means (hollow = day with fewer than 24 hours). Dashed = beyond the observed data; the shaded band is the 95 % prediction interval. Click a legend item to hide it.

How much should you trust the line?

Width of the 95 % band at each projected day, against the day-to-day noise.
